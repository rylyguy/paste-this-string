ln-string-decode

Paste any Bitcoin or Lightning string. Get type, BOLT11 expiry, and whether a Lightning Address LNURL is reachable. Runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Client-side only. LNURL-pay metadata is fetched from the receiver domain (no amount, no invoice mint). Optional tips at the bottom — skip them.

Paste a string, or tap an example.

What it classifies

Looks likeTypeExtra decode
name@domainLightning Address (LUD-16)LNURL-pay fetch: reachable, min/max
lnurl1…LNURL (bech32 URL)Decoded URL + payRequest probe
lnbc… / lntb…BOLT11 invoiceAmount, timestamp, expiry, expired?
lno1…BOLT12 offerReusable (not one-shot)
1 / 3 / bc1q / bc1pOn-chainLightning cannot pay these

Checksums are verified for bech32 (BOLT11 / LNURL). Base58 on-chain is prefix-length only. This is not a wallet.
